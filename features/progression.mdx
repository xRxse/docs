NightGuildBot rewards member activity through player leveling and XP progression. Use this page to understand the supported progression systems and the records you can track.

## Levels And Ranks

The bot supports player levels, XP progression, and rank advancement. Exact XP formulas, qualifying activities, and rank thresholds are not specified in this wiki.

## Tools And Boosts

Custom tools include **swords**, **axes**, and **pickaxes**. The bot also includes boosts that influence progression and rewards. Tool stats, boost values, durations, and combination rules need a confirmed reference before they can be listed here.

## Player Statistics

NightGuildBot tracks individual statistics and progression, including fishing records, fish collections, and catch weights. Leaderboards support competition between players. Ranking criteria are not specified here.
