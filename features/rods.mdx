NightGuildBot uses fishing rods with different stats and abilities. This page introduces rod progression, enchantments, and special rods without assuming how any particular rod is obtained.

## Rod Progression

Different rods offer different capabilities as you progress through fishing. Check the bot's available item information before comparing rods. Exact stats and acquisition requirements have not been supplied for this wiki.

## Enchantments

Fishing enchantments improve rod performance and luck. Available enchantments, effect values, and application requirements still need a confirmed reference.

## Special Rods

The **Cosmic Rod** and **Owner Rod** are special rods. Their individual abilities and access requirements are not specified here. Administrative tools support granting and removing exclusive rods.

## Guild Bonuses

Certain rods provide guild-specific progression boosts. Which rods provide these bonuses and how the boosts work have not yet been specified.
