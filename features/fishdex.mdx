The NightGuildBot Fishdex lets you discover and collect different fish. Use it as a collection goal alongside fishing progression and tracked catch records.

## Discover Different Fish

Fishing includes multiple species and rarity tiers, ranging from common fish to extremely rare **Divine** fish. A complete species list and all intermediate tiers have not yet been supplied for this wiki.

## Follow Your Collection

The bot supports fish collection tracking and fishing records. Every catch has a tracked weight. The exact Fishdex display, entry fields, and collection completion rules are not specified here.

## Compare Fishing Progress

Fishing leaderboards provide a competitive side to collection and progression. Their ranking criteria are not specified, so do not assume collection size, rarity, or weight determines your position.
