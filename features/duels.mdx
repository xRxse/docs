NightGuildBot includes a duel system that lets players challenge one another. Duels add direct player competition to the bot's RPG experience.

## Challenge Another Player

Find the available duel interaction through the bot's Discord command descriptions. Follow its current instructions for selecting another player and submitting a challenge. Exact command syntax has not been supplied for this wiki.

## Check The Rules

Win conditions, challenge acceptance, cooldowns, rewards, and penalties are not specified here. Check the bot's response before taking part rather than assuming these rules.

## Tools And Progression

The RPG system includes swords, axes, and pickaxes, but their role in duels has not been specified. This wiki does not claim any tool is required or changes a duel outcome.

See [Player Progression](/features/progression) for levels, boosts, and statistics.
