NightGuildBot lets players create and manage guilds with their own progression. Guilds combine membership management, upgrades, and activity tracking in one community system.

## Membership And Management

Guild tools support creation, invitations, and officer management. Custom guild roles and permissions help organize members. Exact role capabilities and invitation requirements are not yet specified in this wiki.

## Levels And Upgrades

Guilds have their own XP and leveling system. Guild upgrades provide benefits, but upgrade names, costs, and requirements have not been supplied.

Certain rods provide guild-specific progression boosts. See [Rods](/features/rods) for an introduction to rod progression and special items.

## Activity And Statistics

The bot tracks guild activity and per-guild user statistics. These records let you follow participation and guild progression. The available statistics and their calculation rules need a confirmed reference.

## Administrative Management

Administrative tools support guild resets and progression management. A reset is a potentially destructive operation, so confirm its scope before using it. See [Administration](/commands/admin) for the management overview.
