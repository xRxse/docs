NightGuildBot includes quests, milestones, and special rewards as part of its RPG experience. This page outlines those features without inventing objectives or reward tables.

## Quests

Use the available bot command descriptions and responses to find quest information. Exact command names, objectives, and completion requirements have not been supplied for this wiki.

## Milestones And Rewards

Milestones and special rewards are supported. Their relationship to quests, levels, and other activities has not yet been specified, so this wiki does not assign rewards to particular achievements.

## Before Pursuing An Objective

Check the bot's current instructions for requirements and rewards. NightGuildBot is actively developed, and balancing changes can affect gameplay over time.

For the broader RPG overview, see [Player Progression](/features/progression). Follow [Updates](/resources/changelog) for announcements.
