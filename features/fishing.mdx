NightGuildBot fishing combines rare catches, tracked weights, collecting, and equipment progression. This page explains the supported systems before you explore rods and the Fishdex.

## Species And Rarity

You can catch multiple fish species across several rarity tiers, from common fish to extremely rare **Divine** fish. The full species list, intermediate tiers, and drop rates have not yet been supplied for this wiki.

## Catch Weights

Every catch has a tracked weight. Fishing records and collection tracking support your progress, while fishing leaderboards provide a competitive element. Ranking criteria are not specified here.

## Rewards And Cooldowns

Fishing includes custom rewards, cooldowns, and progression mechanics. Check the bot's current command descriptions and responses for applicable requirements and cooldowns. This wiki does not assume reward amounts or timers.

## Rods And Collection

[Rods](/features/rods) have unique stats and abilities, and enchantments improve performance and luck. The [Fishdex](/features/fishdex) lets you discover and collect different fish.

NightGuildBot is actively developed, so follow [Updates](/resources/changelog) for gameplay and balancing announcements.
